\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Our results show that extending text context length in biomedical VLMs delivers clear gains.
On long-text retrieval tasks, BMC-LongCLIP outperforms prior baselines on both CXR and PMC benchmarks, with the largest gains observed for PMC benchmark.
A key limitation is the scarcity of long-text benchmarks across biomedical domains; expanding such resources will be essential for a fuller evaluation of long-context models.
Taken together, these results establish long-context modeling as a promising direction for advancing biomedical VLMs.
