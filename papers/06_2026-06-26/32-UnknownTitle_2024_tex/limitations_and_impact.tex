\section{Broader Impacts}
\label{lim_and_impacts}
Chart understanding is an especially crucial skill for MLLMs to develop as they are applied to increasingly difficult real-world tasks, such as reading and summarizing scientific papers.
MLLMs with strong chart understanding can analyze and interpret graphs for non-experts to quickly understand and operationalize insights into trends in business, healthcare, and economics.
Therefore, faithful benchmarking of MLLMs is important in the identification and rectification of weaknesses in existing MLLMs.
Our collection of complex, real-world charts is stylistically representative of the types of data MLLMs need to process.
At the time of writing, existing MLLMs struggle to answer chart-related questions reliably, so we believe that \ours{} can meaningfully guide the development and benchmarking of future MLLMs.
