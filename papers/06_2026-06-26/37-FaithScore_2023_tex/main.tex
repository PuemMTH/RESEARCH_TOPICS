\section*{Limitations}
% It's worth noting that, at present, \ours relies on ChatGPT, which can be computationally expensive. Therefore, in the future, researchers can implement this metric using open-source models to make it more accessible and widely applicable. 

\ours\ focuses primarily on factual precision, ensuring that each piece of information in a generated text is supported by the visual input. However, it does not account for factual recall, meaning it doesn't penalize models for generating fewer facts. This can be seen as unfair, as there's often a trade-off between precision and recall. Additionally, the distinction between precision and recall can sometimes be unclear, as a generation may contain supported information but still miss important details. To address this, we suggest reporting \ours\ and the average length of generated text. We leave a more holistic approach for future work.
