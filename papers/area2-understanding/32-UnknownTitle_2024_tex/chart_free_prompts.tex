\section{Chart-Free Random Baseline Prompts}
\label{sec:random_guess_prompt}
We provide the prompts we use for our chart-free random baseline:

\texttt{* Randomly guess a reasonable answer based on the question only. If the question asks for a number, you can randomly guess a number within a reasonable range. If the question asks for a term, you can randomly guess a term that is relevant to the question.}
