\subsection{Downstream Tasks}

In addition to zero-shot evaluation, we also adapt \model\ by finetuning it on a downstream task. We consider four downstream tasks: \emph{(1) Factoid Chart Question Answering:} we use ChartQA \cite{masry-etal-2022-chartqa}, which is a benchmark consisting of factoid question-answer pairs for charts with a particular focus on visual and logical reasoning questions; \emph{(2) Complex Chart Question Answering:} we consider OpenCQA \cite{open-CQA}, another QA benchmark in which answers are explanatory descriptions; \emph{(3) Chart Summarization:}  we use Chart-to-Text \cite{chart-to-text-acl}, a large-scale benchmark for chart summarization; \emph{(4) Chart-to-Table:} we use ChartQA for both finetuning and evaluation. Moreover,  we evaluate the pretrained model in a zero-shot setup on the WebCharts dataset \cite{Choi2019VisualizingFT}, a collection of 300 charts obtained from the web. Our experimental setups and hyperparameters for each downstream task are provided in \Cref{app:details-downstream-tasks}.
